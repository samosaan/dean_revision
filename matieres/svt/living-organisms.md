## Definition

> A **living organism** is a thing that is alive because it can do things like grow, move, reproduce, adapt to its environment, and do life processes like respiration, reproduction…

## I. The 7 characteristics of living things: « MRS GREN »

Le truc pour s'en souvenir : chaque lettre de **MRS GREN** est une caractéristique.

| | Characteristic | Definition (trous complétés) |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | Movement | The ability for an organism to **move** (part or whole body) |
| **R** | Respiration | A set of chemical **reactions** (i.e. converting sugar to energy) |
| **S** | Sensitivity | Ability to detect and respond to the **environment** (i.e. temperature, hydration…) |
| **G** | Growth | Get larger with increase in dry **mass** |
| **R** | Reproduction | Process that **makes** more of the same organism |
| **E** | Excretion | Removal of **waste** produced by the body |
| **N** | Nutrition | Absorbing **materials** from the environment (i.e. food, water, energy) |

*What you need to know: the 7 characteristics, and be able to explain them.*

## II. What are living organisms made of?

> **Definition:** Cells are the smallest unit of life and the building blocks for all organisms.

### II.1 Animal cell

| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| **Nucleus** | Contains the genetic material (DNA) and controls the cell's **activities** |
| **Cytoplasm** | Liquid that makes up most of the cell. Mainly **water** |
| **Cell membrane** | A flexible outer **layer** that controls what passes in or out |
| **Mitochondria** | Small « factories » where energy is released from glucose. Where most **respiration** happens |

### II.2 Plant cell

Same parts as the animal cell (nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane, mitochondria), **plus**:

| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| **Cell wall** | Rigid outer layer containing cellulose, gives **strength** and support |
| **Vacuole** | Space filled with cell sap. Keeps the cell **firm** |
| **Chloroplasts** | Only in green parts of plants. Contain chlorophyll, where **photosynthesis** occurs |

Astuce : la cellule végétale a 3 choses en plus, un **mur** (cell wall), une **poche d'eau** (vacuole) et du **vert** (chloroplasts).
